# Vicinia

**Vicinia** (formerly *CivicLens*) is an AI-powered Community Intelligence Platform that gives every street, neighborhood, city, state, and country a transparent, community-verified, living profile.

> Every place has a story. Vicinia helps the community tell it — and prove it.

## What it is

- **Not a complaint app.** Reporting is one input; the product is *understanding places*.
- **One pin per real-world incident** — duplicates become confirmations, so the map shows truth, not noise.
- **39 issue categories + 12 positive signals** — places get credit for what works, not just blame for what's broken.
- **Full lifecycle with memory** — incidents move from Reported → Verified → Resolved with the entire evidence timeline preserved forever.
- **Vicinia Score** — a transparent 0–100 health score for any geographic unit, from a single street to a country.
- **AI as an assistant, never a judge** — duplicate/fake detection, summaries, trends, and Ask AI, all overridable by community consensus.
